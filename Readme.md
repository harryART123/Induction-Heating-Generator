## Problem 
Is it possible to reach high temperatures efficiently in thermal processes using renewable energy sources?
---
## Theoretical framework :memo:
The heating by induction is based on **Faraday's law** and **Foucault's current (eddy currents)** [1]. When an alternating current of high frequency goes around in a coil (inductor), it generates a variable magnetic field. Whether a conducting material is placed in this field, induced electric currents are generated inside; due to the material's resistance, heat is released (Joule effect):
Key principles:
- **Electromagnetic induction**
- **Foucault's current (eddy currents)**
- **Skin effect**
- **Resonance**
